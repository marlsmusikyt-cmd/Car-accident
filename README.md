# 🚗 Car Accident

A simple 2D arcade driving game where your goal is to avoid incoming traffic and survive as long as possible!

## 🎮 About the Game

**Car Accident** is a small 2D arcade game with a simple pixel-inspired style.

Control your car, dodge other vehicles and try to achieve the highest score possible. The longer you survive, the faster the traffic becomes!

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Slider:** Move your car left and right.
- **Arrow Keys:** Control your car using the keyboard.
- **A / D:** Alternative keyboard controls.

## ✨ Features

- Simple pixel-inspired graphics
- Traffic with randomly spawning cars
- Collision detection
- Increasing difficulty
- Score system
- Best score saved locally
- Simple and easy-to-use controls

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Download or clone the project.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.
4. Start the game.
5. Avoid the other cars and beat your high score!
